# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Ace's owner and family, uploading nap photos from a phone in a few seconds. They are not power users — they want to pick photos, tap upload, and move on.

## Product Purpose

Queue nap photos for [@ace_naps](https://www.instagram.com/ace_naps/). One photo posts each morning at 9 AM Eastern with a random caption. Success means photos land in the queue reliably with zero friction.

## Brand Personality

Personal, calm, trustworthy. A small private tool — not a product launch. Three words: simple, warm, dependable.

## Anti-references

- SaaS marketing dashboards with hero metrics and card grids
- Over-designed upload flows with drag-and-drop theatrics
- Dense admin panels or settings-heavy interfaces this audience will never use

## Design Principles

1. **Phone-first, one task** — the upload screen should work perfectly on mobile with large touch targets.
2. **Obvious feedback** — every action gets a clear success or error message; never leave the user guessing.
3. **Minimal chrome** — no navigation, no features beyond upload; the tool disappears into the task.
4. **Fail gracefully** — validation and upload errors explain what went wrong and how to recover.
5. **Earned familiarity** — use standard Nuxt UI patterns; no custom affordances for standard tasks.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast on all text. Touch targets at least 44×44px. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`. Works with keyboard and screen readers on the upload form.
